About me

I am Dr. Shafqat Ali Siyyal, with a PhD in Autonomous Systems. My research intersects control-theoretic security, constrained optimization and resilience engineering, focusing on the safety and reliability of cyber-physical and autonomous systems.

My work integrates model-based control, robust and predictive control strategies and observer-based anomaly detection to address uncertainties and adversarial disturbances. I also investigate software rejuvenation framework that supports system recovery while preserving operational safety.

My methodological approach combines dynamical system modeling, Lyapunov stability analysis, invariant-set characterization and reachability analysis to evaluate safety constraints and closed-loop performance under disturbances, faults and cyberattacks.
